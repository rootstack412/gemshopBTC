Gem care

Reference page General guidance only — when in doubt, ask us about your specific piece before using chemicals or ultrasonics.

Daily wear

Put jewelry on last

Perfume, lotion, and hairspray can build up under stones. Rings can take a knock when lifting weights or gardening — know when to take them off.

Cleaning

Warm water & soft brush

For many durable gems, a few minutes in lukewarm water with mild soap and a baby-soft toothbrush removes skin oils and dust. Rinse well and dry with a lint-free cloth.

Caution

Heat, steam & ultrasound

Emerald, opal, pearl, tanzanite, and many treated stones can be damaged by rapid temperature change or vibration. Save aggressive methods for pieces your jeweler has cleared.

Storage

Separate soft pouches

Harder gems scratch softer ones. Store pieces so they do not rattle together in a drawer or travel pouch.
