About us

A small team with a big love for natural beauty, honest sourcing, and the moment a piece finds its person.

Mockup copy Beneath the Crescent began with a simple idea: buying a gem or a piece of jewelry should feel special, not rushed. Our showroom is designed for unhurried conversation — loupe in hand if you want it — so you can choose with confidence.

We believe in transparency. When we know a stone’s origin or any treatment it has received, we share it. When we are not sure, we say that too. Trust is part of the sparkle.

What you will find here

  • Loose gemstones and finished jewelry
  • Gift-worthy pieces and serious collector’s items
  • Repairs and sizing coordinated with trusted bench jewelers (details vary — ask in store)
